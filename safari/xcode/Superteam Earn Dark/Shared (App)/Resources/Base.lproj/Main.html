Superteam Earn Dark

Dark mode and full theming for Superteam Earn.

Turn it on in Safari

  1. Open Safari and go to superteam.fun/earn.
  2. Tap the extensions button in the address bar — the puzzle-piece icon. On iOS 16 and 17 it lives inside the AA page settings menu instead.
  3. Tap Manage Extensions, then switch Superteam Earn Dark on.
  4. Tap the extensions button again, choose Superteam Earn Dark, and pick Always Allow → Always Allow on This Website.

Or from the Settings app: Apps › Safari › Extensions › Superteam Earn Dark.
